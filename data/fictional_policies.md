# Northstar Outfitters — Fictional Sources

Northstar Outfitters is an invented retailer used only for this synthetic demonstration.

## Shipping policy

- Standard delivery within Ireland takes three to five business days.
- Standard delivery within Ireland costs EUR 5.
- Orders above EUR 60 qualify for free standard delivery within Ireland.
- International destinations are not represented in this demonstration dataset.

## Product source

- Trail Flask: 750 ml, stainless steel, available in navy and moss.
- Daybreak Pack: 18 litres, recycled polyester, available in black and rust.
- No source in this project guarantees that a product is suitable for medical, safety-critical, or specialist use.

## Authority boundaries

- Only authorized staff can change orders, issue refunds, approve compensation, or resolve disputes.
- Order details require an authenticated customer context.
- When an approved source is absent or conflicting, the workflow must abstain rather than improvise.
